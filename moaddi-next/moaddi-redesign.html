Using Moaddi

Moaddi lets you buy snacks and drinks from smart vending machines by scanning the QR code on any machine. You pay only for items the machine successfully opens — anything that fails to open is refunded automatically to your payment method.

Payments

Payments are processed by Moyasar. Moaddi never stores your card details. Refunds arrive within 5–7 business days depending on your bank.

Your account

Your account is tied to your phone number. You can request deletion of your account and purchase history at any time from Help & support.

Design spec & implementation map

Simple and modern, grounded in the real logo colors. Teal is the only action color; gold appears exclusively on offers, savings, and low-stock flags so it always means "act now". Cairo remains the single typeface (Arabic + Latin), and every layout uses logical start/end properties so RTL mirrors cleanly. Every color is a CSS custom property, so the whole system carries a matching dark theme (toggle it with the icon in the header) instead of being inverted after the fact. The redesign is a re-skin: routes, GraphQL/REST contracts, cart context and the socket purchase flow stay untouched.
